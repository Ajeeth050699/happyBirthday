Happy Birthday, My Love.

My healer Vaishu,

Happy Birthday to the most beautiful, kind-hearted, loving, and precious person I have ever known. Today is the day the world was blessed with someone so special, and I feel like the luckiest man alive because that person became the love of my life.

There are so many things I want to tell you, but no matter how many words I write, they will never be enough to express what you truly mean to me.

First of all, I want to say thank you.

Thank you for loving me with all your heart. Thank you for believing in me when I couldn't believe in myself. Thank you for standing beside me through every difficult moment, every painful day, every failure, every tear, and every struggle. When life became heavy, you became my strength. When I felt lost, you became my light. When I thought nobody understood me, you understood my silence better than my words.

You never walked away from me during my hardest times. Instead, you held my hand tighter and reminded me that we would face everything together. That kind of love is rare, and I promise I will never take it for granted.

Vaishu, you've done so much for me. You bought me things without me asking. You cared for me without expecting anything in return. You always wanted to see me smile, even if it meant sacrificing your own happiness. Your love has always been selfless, pure, and unconditional.

"Sometimes I feel that you love me far more than I deserve — but please never doubt this, I love you with every beat of my heart."

Every day I thank God for bringing you into my life.

There is another person I want to thank from the bottom of my heart — Lalitha. If it wasn't for Lalitha, I may never have met you. The memory of meeting you at Lalitha's home is still one of the most precious moments of my life. I can still remember that day so clearly. At that time, I never imagined that the girl I met there would one day become the love of my life, my best friend, my biggest blessing, and my forever person.

That beautiful memory will always stay in my heart, because it was the beginning of the most beautiful chapter of my life — you.

You are not just my girlfriend. You are my peace after every storm, my safe place, my best friend, my greatest blessing.

You are the person who makes ordinary days feel extraordinary. You are my happiness, my comfort, my strength, my inspiration, and my home.

I don't just want to celebrate your birthdays with you. I want to celebrate every morning beside you. I want to laugh with you until our cheeks hurt. I want to travel the world with you. I want to build a home filled with love, respect, laughter, and countless beautiful memories. I want to hold your hand through every success, every challenge, every joy, and every tear.

I want to grow old with you. I want to see your smile every morning and thank God every night for giving me another day with you. When our hair turns grey and our faces are covered with wrinkles, I still want to look at you with the same love I have today. I want to annoy you, make you laugh, take care of you when you're tired, and remind you every single day that you're the most beautiful woman in my eyes.

I promise that no matter what happens in life, I will never stop choosing you. I promise to respect you. I promise to protect your heart. I promise to support your dreams. I promise to stay beside you during every difficult moment, just as you've always stayed beside me. I promise to love you not only when life is easy but also when life becomes difficult — because real love isn't just about happy moments, it's about staying together through everything.

Thank you for accepting me with all my imperfections. Thank you for never giving up on me. Thank you for making my life brighter simply by being in it.

You have changed my life in ways you'll probably never realise. Because of you, I smile more, dream bigger, and believe that true love really exists.

Vaishu, if I had one wish, it would be to spend the rest of my life with you. I don't need a perfect life — I just need a life with you in it.

No matter where life takes us, no matter how many years pass, no matter what challenges come our way, I will keep loving you with all my heart.

You are my first thought when I wake up and my last thought before I fall asleep. You are the most beautiful chapter of my life. You are my forever. And if I could choose again in another lifetime, I would still choose you. Every single time.

Happy Birthday, my princess, my angel, my soulmate, my forever love.

May God bless you with endless happiness, good health, success, peace, and every beautiful thing your heart desires. I pray that He gives us a long, beautiful life together so I can continue loving you, making memories with you, and growing old by your side.

I love you more than words can ever express.

Forever yours, always and forever —
I will never leave you.
